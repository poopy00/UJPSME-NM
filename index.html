About UJPSME-NM

A regional home for ME students

UJPSME-NM is the regional federation of Junior Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers chapters across Northern Mindanao. It coordinates shared programs, promotes student welfare across chapters, and builds connections between students, alumni, and industry region-wide.

Vision To be the leading regional federation in Mindanao — uniting mechanical engineering students, strengthening every member chapter, and shaping the future of the field across Northern Mindanao.

What We Do

Regional programs

Six working areas, one goal: ME students across Northern Mindanao who are better connected and better prepared.

Shared Programming

Joint seminars, workshops, and training no single chapter could run alone.

Leadership Development

Regional roles, competitions, and projects that build character across chapters.

Industry Exposure

Plant visits and company engagements opened up to every member chapter.

Workshops and Training

Hands-on CAD, design, and engineering-tool sessions run region-wide.

Competitions

Regional quiz bowls, CAD contests, and design meets between chapters.
