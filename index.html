01 — About

A regional home for ME students

UJPSME-NM is the regional federation of Junior Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers chapters across Northern Mindanao. It exists to serve the region's ME student community by coordinating shared programs, promoting student welfare across chapters, and building connections between students, alumni, and industry.

Vision To be the leading regional federation in Mindanao — one that unites mechanical engineering students, strengthens every member chapter, and shapes the future of the field across Northern Mindanao.

Chapter Support

Resources and guidance so every member chapter can run strong programs, regardless of size.

Student Welfare

A welcoming federation where students across the region feel represented and supported.

Technical Skill

Shared training that turns coursework into working engineering ability.

Connection

Real bonds between chapters, alumni, and industry across Northern Mindanao.

02 — What We Do

What the federation actually does

Six working areas, one goal: ME students across Northern Mindanao who are better connected and better prepared.

Shared Programming

Joint seminars, workshops, and training no single chapter could run alone.

Leadership Development

Regional roles, competitions, and projects that build character across chapters.

Industry Exposure

Plant visits and company engagements opened up to every member chapter.

Workshops and Training

Hands-on CAD, design, and engineering-tool sessions run region-wide.

Competitions

Regional quiz bowls, CAD contests, and design meets between chapters.

Community and Outreach

A welcoming federation for every ME student in the region, plus service beyond campus.
